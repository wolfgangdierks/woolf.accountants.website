About

I’m Wolfgang Dierks, CMA. I’ve spent 30+ years in finance leadership, and multiple times I was the first finance hire at a company. So I know what an owner actually wants: no thoughts wasted on numbers, because you’d rather be in the field. No headaches, worry-free books.

I bring big-company job accounting to small businesses: progress billing, retainage, change orders, and WIP, all handled by your own accounting and finance department.

I lead it, and I do the accounting work myself. As your workload grows, I add accounting staff to match, and AI agents handle the repetitive work. You always talk to me.
